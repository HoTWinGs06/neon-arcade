# 🕹️ NEON PULSE: 198X RETRO ARCADE ⚡

> *Insert Coin to Begin...*

Welcome to **NEON PULSE**, an immersive, neon-drenched 198X retro arcade experience built directly with HTML5 Canvas and Web Audio API. 

---

## 🌟 FEATURES

- **CRT Scanline & Glow Overlay:** Authentic 80s arcade cabinet visual styling.
- **Polyphonic Chiptune Jukebox:** Play classic chiptune tracks (*The Entertainer*, *Canon in D*, *Minuet in G*, *Ode to Joy*) while you play!
- **Fluid Touch & Mouse Controls:** Responsive paddle movement designed for both desktop and mobile devices.
- **Power-Ups & Multi-ball:** Catch falling power-ups to expand your paddle or spawn multiple balls.
- **Hidden Easter Eggs:** 
  - 🔓 **God Mode:** Tap the header title 5 times (or enter the Konami Code) for 99 lives!
  - 🎵 **Secret Rickroll:** Tap the "CHIPTUNE" label 5 times... if you dare!

## 🕹️ HOW TO PLAY

1. **Move:** Drag your mouse or touch and drag on mobile across the canvas. (Keyboard controls: Arrow keys / `A` & `D`).
2. **Objective:** Keep the neon ball in play and smash all the retro bricks!
3. **Score:** Rack up points and set a new high score saved locally.
